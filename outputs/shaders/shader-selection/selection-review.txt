The reviewed reference draft is a suitable first live trial without new downloads:
- CLOUD_STYLE_DEFINE=50 is the pack's explicitly documented Vanilla option. It stops shaders.properties from forcing clouds off. Vanilla clouds still require Video Settings clouds enabled; use Fast for the target's flat cloud shape when possible.
- ATM_FOG_MULT=0.50 is the author's Very Subtle setting. BORDER_FOG=false removes horizon whiteout; CAVE_FOG=false prevents haze from flattening cave stone.
- ATM_NOON_R/G/B=0.90/0.95/1.10 shifts atmosphere toward clean blue. LIGHT_NOON_R/G/B=1.10/1.00/0.95 warms direct sunlight moderately.
- SHADOW_SMOOTHING=4 is Very Smooth. SSAO_I=85 and VANILLAAO_I=100 retain object grounding without excessive dark corners.
- CAVE_LIGHTING=120 adds a small amount of neutral/cool fill to unlit areas. XLIGHT_I=1.10 and XLIGHT_CURVE=0.90 improve local torch readability. XLIGHT_R/G/B=1.00/0.95/0.85 makes already-warm pack blocklight slightly warmer.
- BLOOM_STRENGTH=0.072 is author-labeled 40%, below the default 0.12. Motion blur, world blur, chromatic aberration, vignette and lens flare are off. Generated normals and coated textures are off so original texture surfaces remain familiar.
- TAA_DEFINE=1, TAA_SMOOTHING=2 and IMAGE_SHARPENING=2 balance moving-leaf stability with crisp texture detail. The no-temporal-filter draft is a fallback for a measured motion-softness issue, not the first recommendation; the author internally limits shadow quality with temporal filtering disabled.
